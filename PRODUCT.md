# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Novice-to-technical learners working through a complete, source-grounded course on quantum chemistry, quantum algorithms, and resource estimation. Readers need patient explanations, inspectable mathematics and code, and a clear route through the entire workflow.

## Product Purpose

From Molecule to Machine turns 34 full technical lessons into a readable, interactive course. Understanding the end-to-end workflow matters more than finishing a dashboard or collecting a score.

## Positioning

An independent educational project connecting chemistry preparation to quantum representation, execution, validation, and the handoffs to compiler, resource-estimation, error-correction, and hardware specialists. It is not official Microsoft documentation and makes no claim of quantum advantage.

## Operating Context

Publicly hosted at the owner's personal GitHub Pages project site. The user explicitly approved the public repository `Rohit-2002-28/molecule-to-machine` and public access to its lessons and source. Existing technical and PM learning material and upstream QDK repositories remain untouched.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the complete original technical lessons, source references, caveats, and named-method coverage from `src/content/course.json`.
- Provide curriculum and section navigation, full-text search, relevant diagrams, educational mini-labs, lesson checks, glossary, and cross-team handoff explanations.
- Store reading progress, bookmarks, and check results only in the reader's browser, with visible status and clear/reset controls. No accounts, analytics, remote inference, payments, or credential-bearing browser code.
- Render safe Markdown, code, tables, and mathematical notation. Keep the lesson text available independently of interactive enhancements.
- Final publication requires 34 complete, nonplaceholder lessons. Counts and reading estimates derive from actual content.
- Use static hosting with reliable fresh lesson links under the GitHub Pages project base path.
- The user approved the scientific-atlas direction: clear reading pages, cobalt diagrams, and interactive concept plates.

## Brand Commitments

The course title is "From Molecule to Machine." Its voice is technically precise, beginner-friendly, candid about assumptions, and independent of Microsoft endorsement.

## Evidence on Hand

The coordinating session supplies the original course export and verifies references against public `microsoft/qdk-chemistry` revision `97a3ff4fb86ebd5a2516e3548cece69127fd3555`. The initial repository contains only its generated README; there is no existing visual identity or asset library. Do not invent scientific results, users, customers, endorsements, or benchmarks.

## Product Principles

1. A full readable course is enhanced, never replaced, by interactive learning.
2. Educational models state their assumptions and limits; an illustrative calculator is not a scientific calculation or real quantum resource estimate.
3. Reading progress is distinct from knowledge-check results, and nothing is locked behind prerequisites.
4. Every source-based explanation retains provenance and uncertainty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-accessible controls and navigation, visible focus, meaningful text alternatives, reduced-motion support, high contrast, mobile reading without page overflow, independently scrollable code/tables/math, and printable lessons. Storage or chart failures must not hide the course.
